# Carrier onboarding canonical evidence pack

This is a normalized internal corpus seed assembled from approved Kushal/Vorto/
Bainbridge Front messages and recruiter statements in OME calls. It grounds
retrieval; it is not a public policy document. Dynamic terms must be checked
against current source records before being shown to a carrier.

## Application and onboarding

- The registration link is the quickest way to begin. The carrier then completes
  the carrier packet, signs required documents, submits insurance, and completes
  applicable Evident/background-check and RMIS steps.
- After documents are complete, the carrier schedules a group onboarding call
  from the welcome email or arranges a one-to-one call.
- Carriers who have not run with LoHi for roughly 60 days may need onboarding
  again so insurance and other information can be rechecked.

## Insurance and compliance

- Insurance must be on file with the designated MCP system; a COI sent directly
  to recruiting may not satisfy the requirement.
- Recurring Front guidance cites 1M general liability, 1M auto liability, and
  100k cargo coverage. Additional regional certificates such as CARB or Clean
  Truck may be required.
- Front guidance communicates 30-day RMIS and 14-day Evident completion windows
  in the applicable onboarding flow.

## Authority eligibility (conflicting)

- Multiple Front messages state a minimum of six months continuous authority
  (sometimes described as proof of 180 days).
- Some OME recruiter scripts state that newly authorized carriers may start.
- Until a current program owner resolves this conflict, the agent must ask for
  the carrier's program/region and escalate rather than give a universal answer.

## Training (context-dependent)

- Some OME recruiter scripts say formal training is not required for a given flow.
- Front messages also provide mandatory web/driver training links for specific
  programs. The agent must identify the applicable program first.

## Payment, rates, equipment, and bonuses

- Recruiting scripts commonly describe next-business-day pay with no factoring
  fees, while a Front response allows factoring during an initial three-week
  period with a notice of assignment. These are program-specific.
- Recruiting materials commonly cite a 9.75% take rate and power-only or
  pneumatic/sand-box equipment depending on region and lane.
- Trailer credits or rental may be available in some regions; exact terms are
  confirmed during onboarding.
- Sign-on, referral, and shift bonuses vary by region and week. Historical
  amounts must not be presented as current without a fresh source.

## Escalation

- If a carrier cannot complete a step, identify the missing system/document
  (MCP, RMIS, Evident, training, or application status) and route the issue to
  the recruiting/onboarding team or a scheduled call.
