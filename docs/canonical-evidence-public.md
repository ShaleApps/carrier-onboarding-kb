# Carrier onboarding public evidence

Carrier-safe guidance approved for the public retrieval lane. Dynamic rates,
bonus amounts, internal contacts, and conflicting eligibility rules remain
internal until separately confirmed.

## Starting onboarding

- Begin with the registration link supplied by LoHi or Bainbridge.
- Complete the carrier packet and sign the documents presented during
  registration. Submit required insurance and background-check information.
- Once the required steps are complete, use the welcome email to book a group
  onboarding call or request a one-to-one call.

## Checking progress

- Use the registration-status link supplied by email or SMS to view current
  application progress.
- The status page may provide links for Tenstreet enrollment, the Tenstreet
  portal (including W-9 completion when applicable), RMIS registration, and
  onboarding-call booking.
- If a status remains pending, follow the specific missing-step instruction on
  the status page and contact the recruiting team if the instruction is unclear.

## Verification and documents

- Insurance must be submitted through the channel specified in the onboarding
  instructions; a document sent to the wrong destination may not update status.
- Complete RMIS, Evident/background-check, and any training steps shown for your
  program. Requirements can vary by brokerage, region, or equipment.
- Do not send sensitive documents in chat unless the onboarding instructions
  explicitly direct you to do so.

## Getting help

- For a blocked step, identify the system or document named on the status page
  (for example, insurance, RMIS, Evident, training, or Tenstreet) and contact
  the recruiting/onboarding team through the channel in your welcome email.
